Source Authority Hierarchy (Levels 1–5)

Answers on the platform are weighted strictly according to statutory hierarchy:

  • LEVEL 1: Primary Legislation, Official Gazettes, Patent Offices, Central Acts (e.g. The Patents Act 1970, Biological Diversity Act 2002).
  • LEVEL 2: Official Regulatory Guidelines, TKDL Protocols, AYUSH Pharmacopoeial Standards.
  • LEVEL 3: Official International Organizations (WIPO Lex, WTO TRIPS, WHO, Nagoya Protocol).
  • LEVEL 4: Institutional Research & Certified Academic Repositories.
  • LEVEL 5: Secondary Web Sources (used solely for general corroboration, never for binding legal citations).
